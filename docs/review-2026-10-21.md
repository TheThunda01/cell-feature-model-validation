## Scientific assessment

UCI and the versioned scikit-learn description confirm that these are 569 image-derived records with 30 nuclear-feature summaries. They are not genomic observations. The historical 114-record holdout remains explicitly previously inspected. The cross-validation and threshold descriptions match the code: tuning occurs inside each outer training fold, while threshold choice reuses cross-fitted development labels and therefore reports tuning loss.

Current PubMed abstracts for Varma and Simon, Van Calster et al., Lange and Brunner, and Murad et al. support the distinctions between tuning and assessment, discrimination and calibration, and conditional predictive values. The official Lipton et al. abstract confirms the label-shift definition. This follow-up does not claim a fresh full-text or supplementary appraisal of those sources.

The PPV/NPV equations, positive-class direction, fixed-rate assumptions, and pointwise calibration-interval explanation were checked against the implementation and stored counts. No scientific wording required a change to their interpretation. A complete feature-map declaration still leaves semantic compatibility unestablished; it is not approval of external validation.
