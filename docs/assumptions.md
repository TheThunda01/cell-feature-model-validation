# Assumptions and their consequences

These are the material assumptions and deliberate choices in the current implementation. A check can verify a rule without establishing its scientific truth. Terms are defined in the [glossary](glossary.md); source details and access limits are in the [reference audit](reference-audit.json).

| Assumption or choice | Rationale and evidence | What is checked or remains untested | Consequence if it fails or is generalized |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The bundled WDBC copy is the intended dataset. | UCI and scikit-learn document 569 records with 30 nuclear-image summaries. A fixed copy avoids silent source substitution. | Shape, finite values and input fingerprint are checked. Malignant is explicitly recoded to 1. | Archived labels and acquisition quality are not independently re-established; this is not a representative screening cohort. |
| Rows are usable resampling units. | The available matrix supports record-level splits, but lacks adequate site/time/participant relationship fields for richer grouping. | Row-level separation is tested; biological independence is not established from the bundled fields. | Correlated observations or unrecorded collection structure may limit uncertainty estimates and transfer. |
| The original split should be preserved. | Version 0.1 already exposed the 114-record holdout. Keeping its indices documents history; replacing it would not erase prior learning from this dataset. | A constant index hash enforces the 455/114 split. | Later holdout scores are descriptive replication, never a fresh blind test. |
| Repeated nested CV is useful internal assessment. | Varma and Simon (2006) motivate separating tuning from assessment. Four outer folds, two repeats and three inner folds are a feasible chosen design. | Scaling/tuning exclusion is tested for each outer evaluation role. | Fold scores overlap and are not independent replications; they do not establish external validity. |
| The limited model families and parameter grids are sufficient for a comparison. | Logistic regression, a small random forest and a class-prior baseline provide different simple reference behaviors. The grids are practical choices, not proven optimal settings. | All candidates and selected values are saved; both families are retained. | Mean score differences do not establish universal superiority or a mechanistic explanation. |
| Mean cross-fitted probabilities are useful for illustrating cutoff selection. | Each record’s two predictions were made without its own fitting/tuning role. Their average stabilizes the development score used by the fixed grid. | Exclusion and penalties/tie-breaking are tested. | The same labels choose the cutoff; minimized loss is tuning loss. Averaging smaller models need not match the final refit’s probability distribution. |
| FN:FP penalties 1:1, 2:1 and 5:1 are illustrative. | They make competing errors explicit. They were not elicited from patients or a clinical decision analysis. | The threshold grid and tie rules are fixed in the code. | No cutoff is a clinical recommendation; the best choice depends on consequences and population. |
| Fixed-prediction bootstrap intervals are descriptive. | 1,000 historical-holdout resamples quantify one conditional sampling component. | Single-class resamples are discarded. No model is refitted. | Intervals omit development, tuning, human adaptive reuse, dependence and site/time shifts. |
| Within-bin observations can support binomial summaries. | Wilson (1927) provides an interval for a fraction. Five quantile bins are a transparent display; ties stay together. | Counts and bin definitions are exported; independence is assumed, not verified. | Pointwise intervals do not cover the whole calibration curve or include model/bin-edge uncertainty. |
| Sensitivity and specificity stay fixed in the prevalence scenarios. | Bayes’ rule then determines predictive values. Full label shift is a sufficient conceptual setting, but not demonstrated; Murad et al. (2023) caution against universal transportability. | Arithmetic and undefined-value behavior are tested. Rates come from 42 positive and 72 negative historical-holdout records. | Curves are conditional point scenarios, not new data, prevalence estimates, uncertainty bands or individual recalibration. |
| Operator declarations are accurate enough to review, not automatically trust. | Source, measurement and mapping fields record what a reviewer claims. Luijken et al. (2022) motivate checking measurement differences. | Types, completeness, file binding and exact overlap are checked. Scientific truth and approximate overlap remain unverified. | A complete map never establishes semantic compatibility, independence or suitability for external evaluation. |
| Pinned software and finite-precision comparisons support reproducibility. | Versions, seeds and fingerprints identify the analysis. CSV serialization can vary at immaterial precision across systems. | Tests and numerical reproduction use stated tolerances, including within-run provenance checks. | Successful execution and guideline-inspired reporting do not certify clinical utility, fairness or low risk of bias. |

The existing [methods](methods.md) and [practical-use guide](practical-use.md) define operational behavior. These assumptions do not add new experiments or change the preserved results.
